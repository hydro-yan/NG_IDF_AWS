What you are looking at. CESM generates new 2034-2065 weather from physics. The baseline panels are the model's simulation of the past, shown only as the reference the future panels are measured against.

How to use these maps. Use the change, not the absolute numbers. Apply it to the observed value on the Daymet page:
  • IDF — percent change. Multiply: observed 2.0 in × (1 + 20/100) = 2.4 in.
  • ALT — absolute change (ft). Add: observed 3.0 ft + 0.5 ft = 3.5 ft.

Use the ensemble-mean change as the design value, and see the four individual-member maps below to check how much it could vary.
