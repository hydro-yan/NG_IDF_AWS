Methods used in this NG-IDF Tool:
Meteorological forcing: Livneh daily CONUS near-surface gridded meteorological from 1950-2013 at 6-km grid cell
Snow model: Calibrated DHSVM snow model (Sun et al., 2019)
Monthly changes of LAI: MODIS LAI products
IDF frequency analysis: L-moments (Yan et al., 2018)
IDF uncertainty analysis: Monte Carlo ensemble (Yan et al., 2020)
