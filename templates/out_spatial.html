What you are looking at: These maps show current-weather design values based on gridded historical observations (Daymet, 1990–2021), derived from interpolated weather station records.

Which value should I use?
  • NG-IDF (New Method): Integrates cold-region hydrologic processes (snowmelt & runoff) for risk assessment.
  • PREC-IDF (Standard Baseline): Traditional precipitation-only values, provided as a standard baseline to compare against NG-IDF.
  • % Difference: Positive values suggest potential under-design if following the standard PREC-IDF method. E.g., a +20% difference suggests the NG-IDF value is 20% higher than the PREC-IDF value.
Future Scenario Calculations: Future scenario pages report relative/asbosulte change (deltas). To calculate a future design value, apply that delta to the historical NG-IDF or ALT values shown here.
